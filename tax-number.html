SARS & tax

Your SARS Tax Number

Your income tax reference number identifies your tax record with SARS. It is not the same as your ID number.

OverviewTax numberIRP5eFilingReturnsAuto-assessment

When you may need it

Employers, tax returns and some financial processes may ask for your tax number. Many employees are registered through employer processes, but you should still know how to confirm your own status.

Where to check

Use official SARS channels such as eFiling, the SARS MobiApp or other current SARS self-service options. Avoid giving tax details to random people offering to 'fix' your profile.

Keep it private

A tax number is not a password, but it is still personal information. Share it only when there is a legitimate reason.
