Education & career

Learnerships

Learnerships combine structured learning with workplace experience and can lead to a registered qualification.

OverviewUniversityTVETLearnershipsFundingInternshipsUpskilling

Why they can help

They give you both learning and practical exposure, which can be valuable when employers want experience.

Read the advert carefully

Check the qualification, duration, location, entry requirements, stipend or pay information and who is actually offering the programme.

Beware fake opportunities

Verify learnership adverts using official employer, SETA or government channels. Be suspicious of fees to secure placement.

Fictional example · Ayanda

A 12-month learnership offer

Ayanda checks the qualification name, provider, workplace, duration and stipend before accepting. She verifies the opportunity on official channels instead of trusting only the WhatsApp advert.

Key idea: A learnership should be verifiable and linked to real structured learning — not just a vague promise of a job.
