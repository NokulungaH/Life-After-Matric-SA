Upskilling While You Work

You do not always need another full qualification. Sometimes a targeted skill closes the exact gap between you and the next opportunity.

OverviewUniversityTVETLearnershipsFundingInternshipsUpskilling

Start from job adverts

Look at real roles you want and identify skills that appear repeatedly. That is more useful than collecting random certificates.

Choose credible learning

Compare course outcomes, practical work, assessment and recognition. A portfolio or project can sometimes prove more than a certificate alone.

Build evidence

Apply the skill in a project, volunteer role or work task so you can explain what you actually did rather than only saying you completed a course.

Review the return

Before spending heavily, ask whether the course meaningfully improves access to roles, pay or progression in your field.
