Salary & payslips

Deductions & Benefits

A lower net salary does not automatically mean money was 'taken'. Some deductions fund benefits, while others are tax or agreed payroll deductions.

OverviewPayslipPAYE & UIFDeductionsOvertime & bonuses

Retirement contributions

Pension, provident or retirement-annuity contributions can reduce cash in hand while building long-term savings. Understand whether the employer also contributes and whether the quoted salary is basic pay or total cost to company.

Medical aid

If medical aid is part of your employment package, find out who pays what, which plan you are on and whether dependants cost extra.

Other benefits

Group life cover, disability cover, funeral benefits, travel allowances or employer-provided equipment can appear differently across employers. Ask for the benefit schedule if one exists.

Authorised deductions

Do not ignore unexplained lines. Ask what the deduction is, why it applies and whether it is required by law, part of your contract or separately authorised.
