Saving & Investing Basics

Saving is usually for nearer-term certainty. Investing is usually for longer-term growth and comes with risk.

OverviewBudgetingEmergency fundCreditDebtSaving & investing

Match the tool to the goal

Money for next month’s rent should not take the same risk as money for retirement decades away. Start with the goal and time horizon.

Understand risk

Investments can fall in value. A higher expected return usually comes with more uncertainty. Diversification helps avoid relying on one company or one asset.

TFSA basics

A Tax-Free Savings Account has South African tax advantages but also contribution rules and limits. Check current SARS rules before contributing or withdrawing.

Retirement

Starting early gives contributions more time to compound. If your employer offers a retirement fund, understand both your contribution and the employer’s contribution before comparing jobs only on take-home pay.
