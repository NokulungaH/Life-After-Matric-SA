Jobs & applications

Getting into the working world

Practical steps, not “just network” advice.

📄

Build a strong CV

Keep it clear, tailored and easy to scan. Lead with relevant skills, projects, qualifications and measurable outcomes.

  • Use a professional email address
  • Tailor keywords to the job advert
  • Do not include unnecessary personal details
  • Proofread names, dates and contact details
📨

Apply strategically

Quality matters more than firing the same CV everywhere.

  • Save the job description
  • Track the date and role
  • Follow instructions exactly
  • Follow up politely when appropriate
🎤

Prepare for interviews

Know your examples before you walk in.

  • Prepare a 60-second introduction
  • Use STAR for experience questions
  • Research the company
  • Prepare two thoughtful questions
🚨

Spot job scams

Be suspicious if a “recruiter” pressures you to pay for a job, sends vague offers, or asks for sensitive information too early.

A legitimate hiring process should be verifiable. Pause and verify the company independently before sharing documents or money.
