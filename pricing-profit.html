Pricing & Profit

A price should cover more than the supplier cost. It needs to reflect all the costs involved in making the sale happen.

OverviewBasicsPricingCIPCTaxRecordsCalculator

Know landed cost

For products, include purchase cost, shipping, duties or import charges where applicable, packaging and platform or payment fees.

Add operating costs

Advertising, transport, data, storage and returns may not belong to one product but still have to be paid from the margin.

Margin vs markup

Markup is added to cost; margin is profit as a percentage of selling price. They are not the same calculation.

Test the market

A mathematically profitable price is not useful if customers will not pay it. Compare your total cost with realistic market pricing before buying large quantities.
