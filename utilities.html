Utilities & Household Costs

The first electricity token is only one of the costs that appear once you run your own home.

OverviewRentingLeaseAffordabilityUtilitiesScams

Electricity and water

Ask how each service is metered and billed. Prepaid electricity makes usage visible immediately, while municipal or landlord billing can arrive later.

Internet and connectivity

Check installation fees, router costs, contract periods and cancellation terms. Mobile data can be a temporary solution but may be expensive for heavy use.

Groceries and household basics

Cleaning products, toiletries, cooking basics and replacement items are easy to underestimate because they are not bought on the same day every month.

Furniture can wait

You do not need to furnish an entire home immediately. Prioritise sleep, food preparation, safety and basic hygiene before decorative purchases.
