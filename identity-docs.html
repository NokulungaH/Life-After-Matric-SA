Identity & Important Documents

Many adult admin tasks become easier when you know which documents are sensitive, where to get them and how to keep copies safely.

OverviewDocumentsBankingInsuranceConsumerWorkplaceWillsPrivacy

South African ID

Your identity document is used across employment, banking, education and government services. Protect copies because they can be abused for impersonation.

Proof of address

Banks and other institutions may request recent proof showing where you live. Acceptable documents vary, so check the institution’s own list.

Certified copies

Some processes require certified copies rather than ordinary scans. Confirm how recent certification must be before making multiple copies unnecessarily.

Store securely

Keep digital backups somewhere secure and do not leave unprotected copies of IDs, bank statements or payslips on shared devices.
