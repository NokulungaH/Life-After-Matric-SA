SARS & tax

Auto-Assessments

SARS may use third-party data to calculate an assessment without waiting for you to complete a traditional return.

OverviewTax numberIRP5eFilingReturnsAuto-assessment

Do not assume it is automatically correct

Check employment income, medical information, retirement contributions and other relevant data against your own records.

If information is missing

Follow the current SARS process for correcting or submitting information. Deadlines and procedures can change from one filing season to another.

Refunds and amounts owing

A refund is not 'free money'; it generally means more tax was paid than the final calculation required. An amount owing means the final assessed liability exceeds tax already credited.

For current filing dates, thresholds and auto-assessment procedures, always check SARS directly because these change by tax year.
