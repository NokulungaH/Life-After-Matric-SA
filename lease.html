Lease Agreements

A lease is the written record of the rental deal. Read it before you transfer the deposit, not after something goes wrong.

OverviewRentingLeaseAffordabilityUtilitiesScams

Money terms

Check rent, deposit, due date, increases, penalties and which utilities or charges are your responsibility.

Inspections

Document the condition of the property at the start and end. Photos and written inspection records can help avoid disputes about pre-existing damage.

Notice and cancellation

Understand the notice period and what the agreement says about early cancellation, renewal and moving out.

House rules

Check rules on guests, pets, parking, noise, subletting and alterations before assuming they are allowed.

Fictional example · Sipho

“One calendar month’s notice”

Sipho assumes giving notice on 15 June means he can leave on 15 July. But the wording in his lease may mean notice runs by full calendar months, so the practical end date could be different.

Key idea: Do not guess what contract wording means. Ask before signing, especially for notice, cancellation fees, increases and deposits.
