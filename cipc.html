CIPC Basics

CIPC is where South African companies and certain intellectual-property matters are administered. Registration is one part of running a business, not the whole business.

OverviewBasicsPricingCIPCTaxRecordsCalculator

Sole trader vs company

You can earn business income without automatically having a private company. The right structure depends on risk, ownership, tax, administration and growth plans.

Company registration

A registered company becomes a separate legal entity with its own compliance responsibilities. Keep company details and beneficial ownership information current where required.

Directors and records

Directors have legal responsibilities. Do not add people as directors merely to open an account or satisfy a form without understanding what the role means.

Use official channels

Use CIPC’s official systems or a verifiable professional. Be cautious of services that imply registration alone guarantees tenders, funding or tax benefits.
