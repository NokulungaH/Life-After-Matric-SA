Money

Credit Explained

Credit is borrowed purchasing power. It can be useful, but the price is more than the amount you originally spend.

OverviewBudgetingEmergency fundCreditDebtSaving & investing

Credit record

Your credit history reflects how certain credit agreements are managed. Paying on time and not overextending yourself matters more than trying to collect as many accounts as possible.

Credit cards and store accounts

These are not extra income. If you carry a balance, interest and fees can make an ordinary purchase cost significantly more over time.

Before taking credit

Look at the total cost, interest rate, fees, repayment period and what happens if your income changes. Affordability is about the whole budget, not whether the lender approves you.
