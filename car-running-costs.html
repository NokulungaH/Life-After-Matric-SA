Cars

The Real Running Cost of a Car

Even after the instalment is paid, the car keeps costing money every month and every year.

OverviewFinanceBalloonInsuranceRunning costsTrade-insCalculator

Fuel

Estimate from your real kilometres, fuel consumption and fuel price rather than using a generic number. A longer commute can erase the saving from a cheaper instalment.

Maintenance

Service plans do not cover everything. Tyres, wear items, punctures, batteries and repairs can still become your responsibility.

Licensing and admin

Budget for annual licence renewal and any inspection or admin costs that may apply over time.

Depreciation

Cars usually lose value. This matters most when you want to sell or trade while finance is still outstanding.
