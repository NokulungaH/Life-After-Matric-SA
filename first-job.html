Starting work

Your First Job

Your first few weeks are mostly about understanding how this workplace works — and knowing what should be clear from the beginning.

Overview CV Applying Interviews Contracts Job scams First job
Before day one

Confirm the basics

  • Start date and arrival time
  • Office, site or remote-working instructions
  • Dress expectations if relevant
  • Documents HR needs
  • Your signed employment terms
First week

Learn the system

  • Who you report to
  • How work is assigned and reviewed
  • How leave and absence are reported
  • Where workplace policies live
  • How performance is measured
First payday

Check your payslip

  • Your name and pay period
  • Gross remuneration
  • PAYE and UIF where applicable
  • Any other deductions
  • The actual amount paid
Your payslip matters

Department of Employment and Labour guidance states that workers must receive written pay information when paid, including total salary or wages, deductions and the actual amount paid.

Good early habits
  • Keep your contract and payslips
  • Ask questions when instructions are unclear
  • Write down important processes
  • Be on time and communicate delays
  • Ask for feedback instead of guessing
If something does not match

If your pay, deductions, hours or role differ from what you expected, raise the question promptly and keep written records of important discussions. For employment-rights disputes, official Department of Employment and Labour and CCMA resources are useful starting points.

Official references: Basic Guide to Pay Slips and South African Government — Finding a job. Last content review: September 2026.
