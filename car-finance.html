Cars

Vehicle Finance

A car loan spreads the purchase price over time, but interest and fees mean the total paid can be far more than the sticker price.

OverviewFinanceBalloonInsuranceRunning costsTrade-insCalculator

Deposit

A deposit reduces the amount you need to finance. It can lower the instalment and total interest, but do not wipe out your emergency fund just to force a larger deposit.

Interest rate

A lower rate can materially change the total cost over a long term. Compare the rate, fees and term rather than only asking for the monthly instalment.

Term length

A longer term usually lowers the monthly repayment but can increase the total interest paid and keep you in debt for longer.

Total cost

Ask for the total amount repayable, not just the instalment. Finance cost and ownership cost are two different things.

Fictional example · Sipho

Same car, different term

Sipho finances R200 000. A longer term can lower the monthly repayment, but interest has more time to accumulate.

Key idea: A cheaper monthly instalment can be a more expensive loan overall. Compare the total amount repayable and the date the debt actually ends.
