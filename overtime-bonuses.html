Salary & payslips

Overtime, Bonuses & Leave

Extra pay is not always calculated the way people expect. Know what your employment terms say before relying on it.

OverviewPayslipPAYE & UIFDeductionsOvertime & bonuses

Overtime

Whether overtime applies, when it is payable and at what rate depends on the law, your role and your employment terms. Check the applicable policy rather than assuming every extra hour creates overtime pay.

Bonuses

A bonus may be guaranteed, discretionary or linked to performance. Check the wording in your contract or policy and remember a gross bonus can also affect tax withheld in that pay period.

Commission

Commission rules should explain what counts as a sale, when commission is earned and when it is paid. Keep copies of targets and incentive rules.

Leave and pay

Different leave types can affect payroll differently. If you see an unexpected leave deduction, compare it with your leave records and employment policy.
