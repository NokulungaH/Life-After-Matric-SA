Car Insurance Basics

Insurance protects against financial losses you may not be able to absorb yourself. The cheapest premium is not automatically the best cover.

OverviewFinanceBalloonInsuranceRunning costsTrade-insCalculator

Premium

The premium is what you pay for cover. It can be influenced by the vehicle, driver profile, area, use, security and other underwriting factors.

Excess

The excess is the amount you may need to contribute when claiming. A low premium with a very high excess can still be difficult when something goes wrong.

Type of cover

Understand whether cover is comprehensive, third-party only or another form. Financed vehicles commonly have insurance requirements from the finance provider.

Read exclusions

Know what is not covered, how regular drivers must be listed and what security or tracking conditions apply.
