Build a Monthly Budget

A budget is simply a plan for where your money needs to go before the month gets away from you.

OverviewBudgetingEmergency fundCreditDebtSaving & investing

Start with take-home pay

Use the amount that actually reaches your account, not your gross salary. If income changes month to month, build around a conservative estimate rather than your best month.

Separate needs, commitments and wants

Housing, transport, food, debt repayments and essential services usually come first. Then add savings goals and flexible spending.

Leave breathing room

A budget that allocates every last rand can fail the first time something unexpected happens. A small buffer makes the plan more realistic.
