SARS & tax

Your IRP5 Explained

An IRP5 is an employee tax certificate that summarises employment income, deductions and employees’ tax information for a tax year.

OverviewTax numberIRP5eFilingReturnsAuto-assessment

Why it matters

SARS can use employer-submitted IRP5 information when preparing your tax return or auto-assessment. It is one of the key records connecting payroll to your tax profile.

What to check

Check your personal details, employer, tax year, income codes and PAYE totals. If something appears wrong, the employer may need to correct information submitted to SARS.

Keep a copy

Store your IRP5 with other tax records for the relevant tax year, especially if you change employers.
