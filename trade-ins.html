Trade-Ins & Settlement Balances

When a financed car is traded in, the finance must still be settled. The trade value and settlement balance decide whether you have equity or a shortfall.

OverviewFinanceBalloonInsuranceRunning costsTrade-insCalculator

Settlement balance

This is what must be paid to close the existing finance agreement at that time. It is not always equal to the sum of remaining instalments.

Positive equity

If the trade value is higher than settlement, the difference can potentially contribute toward the next vehicle deal.

Negative equity

If settlement is higher than trade value, the shortfall still exists. Adding that shortfall to new finance makes the new car more expensive before you even drive it.

Do the arithmetic yourself

Ask for the trade value, settlement and new finance amount separately. A single monthly instalment can hide what is happening to old debt.

Fictional example · Sipho

Trading in with negative equity

Finance settlementR230 000
Dealer trade offerR190 000
ShortfallR40 000

Key idea: The old car has not disappeared financially. If the R40 000 shortfall is added to the next loan, Sipho starts the new car already owing money from the old one.
