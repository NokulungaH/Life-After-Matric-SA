Emergency Funds

An emergency fund is money kept for genuine unexpected costs so every problem does not have to become debt.

OverviewBudgetingEmergency fundCreditDebtSaving & investing

Start smaller than the internet tells you

If three or six months of expenses feels impossible, start with a smaller milestone. A R1 000 buffer is more useful than waiting years to build the 'perfect' fund.

Base it on essentials

Think about the costs you would still need if income stopped: housing, food, transport, electricity, medication and minimum debt payments.

Keep it accessible

Emergency money should generally be somewhere safe and reasonably easy to access. It is not the same as money invested for a goal ten years away.
