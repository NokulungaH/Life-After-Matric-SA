Banking Admin

A bank account is more than the card in your wallet. Learn the admin behind payments, statements and debit orders.

OverviewDocumentsBankingInsuranceConsumerWorkplaceWillsPrivacy

Proof of banking

Employers and other institutions may ask for proof of banking. Get it from your bank’s official app, branch or online banking rather than editing screenshots.

Statements

Bank statements help you track income, debit orders and spending. Review them regularly instead of only checking the available balance.

Debit orders

Know which companies have permission to debit your account and on what date. Query unfamiliar deductions quickly.

Fees

Compare account fees, cash withdrawal charges, transfer costs and interest rather than choosing an account only because the monthly fee looks low.
