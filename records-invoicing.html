Records & Invoicing

Simple records from day one can save hours of confusion later.

OverviewBasicsPricingCIPCTaxRecordsCalculator

Separate business activity

Even before a dedicated business bank account is necessary, keep a clear record of business money in and money out so personal spending does not hide performance.

Use consistent invoices

Number invoices, date them, identify the customer, describe what was sold, show the amount and keep copies. Add tax information only when legitimately applicable.

Keep expense proof

Save receipts and invoices for genuine business costs and note what each expense was for if it will not be obvious later.

Reconcile regularly

A short weekly or monthly check is much easier than trying to understand twelve months of transactions at year end.
