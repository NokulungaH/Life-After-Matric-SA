Renting Your First Place

Before paying a deposit, make sure you know who you are dealing with, what you are renting and what the monthly cost really includes.

OverviewRentingLeaseAffordabilityUtilitiesScams

Ask what is included

Confirm whether water, electricity, parking, refuse, internet or levies are included in rent or billed separately.

View before paying

Where possible, inspect the property and verify the person or agency you are dealing with. Do not let artificial urgency push you into sending money before basic checks.

Budget beyond rent

Moving can require a deposit, first month’s rent, transport, utility setup, groceries and basic household items at almost the same time.

Fictional example · Sipho

The R4 800 flat is not really R4 800

Key idea: Ask what is included before comparing two properties. A lower rent can still produce a higher monthly housing cost.
