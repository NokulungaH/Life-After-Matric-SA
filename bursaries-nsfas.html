Education & career

Bursaries & NSFAS

Funding rules can change, so use this page to understand the categories and then confirm current eligibility on the official source.

OverviewUniversityTVETLearnershipsFundingInternshipsUpskilling

NSFAS

NSFAS provides funding support to eligible students at public universities and TVET colleges under current programme rules. Eligibility, household-income criteria and application processes can change.

Bursaries

Bursaries may be offered by companies, government departments, professional bodies or foundations. Some have work-back obligations or field-of-study restrictions.

Read conditions

Understand what the funding covers, renewal requirements, academic conditions and whether you must work for the funder afterward.

Use official sources

For NSFAS, use the official NSFAS website. For bursaries, verify the funder’s own website before sending identity documents or banking information.

Funding eligibility and application periods change. Always confirm current NSFAS and bursary rules directly with the official provider.
