Can I Afford to Move Out?

Rent alone does not tell you whether moving out is affordable. Add the rest before signing a lease.

OverviewRentingLeaseAffordabilityUtilitiesScams

Fixed monthly costs

Rent, transport, debt repayments, insurance, internet and recurring subscriptions can leave little room even before food and electricity.

Variable essentials

Groceries, prepaid electricity, toiletries and transport can change from month to month, so do not budget them at their absolute minimum.

Upfront costs

Deposit, first month’s rent, moving transport and household basics can make the first month far more expensive than later months.
