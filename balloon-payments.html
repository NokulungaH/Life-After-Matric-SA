Balloon Payments

A balloon payment leaves part of the vehicle price to the end of the finance agreement, which can make the monthly instalment look more affordable.

OverviewFinanceBalloonInsuranceRunning costsTrade-insCalculator

Why the instalment is lower

You are not repaying the full financed amount through monthly instalments. A lump sum remains at the end.

The future problem

At the end you still need to settle, refinance or otherwise deal with the balloon. The car’s market value may not neatly match what is still owed.

Trade-in risk

If the settlement amount is higher than the trade-in value, you have negative equity. Rolling that shortfall into another car can make the next deal more expensive.
