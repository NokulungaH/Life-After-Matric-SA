Salary & payslips

How to Read a Payslip

A payslip is your record of what you earned, what was deducted and what you were actually paid.

OverviewPayslipPAYE & UIFDeductionsOvertime & bonuses

Start at the top

Check your name, employer, employee number if used, pay period and payment date. If something basic is wrong, raise it early because those details can affect payroll records.

Gross pay

Gross pay is the amount before deductions. It can include basic salary plus items such as overtime, commission, allowances or bonuses depending on your job.

Deductions

Common deductions may include PAYE, UIF, retirement contributions, medical aid or other authorised deductions. Do not assume every deduction is compulsory just because it appears on a payslip.

Net pay

Net pay is the amount left after deductions. Compare it with what actually entered your bank account and query differences promptly.

Keep your payslips.

They are useful proof of income and employment and can help when checking tax records, applying for finance or resolving payroll questions.
