SARS & tax

Tax Returns

A tax return is information submitted to SARS about income, deductions and other relevant tax information for a tax year.

OverviewTax numberIRP5eFilingReturnsAuto-assessment

Do you always file?

Not everyone has the same filing obligation. SARS publishes filing-season rules and thresholds, so check the current year rather than relying on an old social-media post.

Before submitting

Compare pre-populated employment information with your IRP5s and supporting documents. Make sure income from more than one employer or other taxable sources is not accidentally ignored.

After submission

SARS issues an assessment showing the tax calculation. It may result in no balance, a refund or an amount payable.

Keep supporting records

Keep documents that support information you declare. SARS may request verification even after a return is submitted.
