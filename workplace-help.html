Where to Get Workplace Help

When something at work feels wrong, start by identifying whether the problem is payroll, a contract issue, unfair treatment or a formal labour dispute.

OverviewDocumentsBankingInsuranceConsumerWorkplaceWillsPrivacy

Internal first steps

For ordinary mistakes, start with your manager, HR or payroll and explain the issue clearly in writing. Keep copies of relevant documents.

Department of Employment and Labour

The Department provides information and services relating to labour law, basic conditions, UIF and workplace matters.

CCMA

The Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration handles many employment disputes. Time limits can apply, so do not delay if a formal dispute may exist.

Do not rely on social media alone

Online advice can help you identify questions, but formal employment disputes should be checked against official guidance or professional advice.

Fictional example · Thando

His payslip says one thing, his bank account another

Thando’s payslip shows net pay of R12 400, but only R11 600 reaches his bank account. He first checks whether there are multiple payments, then emails payroll with the payslip and bank proof. If the issue is not resolved, he escalates through HR or the appropriate official labour channel.

Key idea: Start with the simplest explanation, keep evidence, and escalate in writing rather than relying on repeated verbal conversations.
