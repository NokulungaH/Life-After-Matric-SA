Job search

Prepare for Interviews

You do not need memorised speeches. You need clear examples that show how you think, communicate and solve problems.

Overview CV Applying Interviews Contracts Job scams First job
01

Research enough to understand the role

Know what the company does, what the team appears to do and what the advert says you will be responsible for. Prepare a reason you are interested that goes beyond “I need a job.”

02

Prepare your examples

Think of examples from work, university, projects, volunteering or life responsibilities that show problem-solving, teamwork, learning, handling pressure and taking ownership.

03

Use STAR when it helps

Situation: what was happening. Task: what you needed to do. Action: what you personally did. Result: what happened and what you learned.

Questions to prepare for
  • Tell me about yourself.
  • Why are you interested in this role?
  • Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.
  • Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
  • How do you prioritise when several things are urgent?
  • What do you want to learn next?
Questions you can ask
  • What would success look like in the first three months?
  • What does a typical week look like?
  • How does the team work together?
  • What are the next steps in the process?
For video or online interviews:

Test your audio, camera and connection beforehand. Keep your device charged, use a quiet space where possible and have the job advert and a few notes nearby.
