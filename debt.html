Understand Debt Before It Grows

Debt becomes difficult when repayments compete with essentials or new borrowing is needed to repay old borrowing.

OverviewBudgetingEmergency fundCreditDebtSaving & investing

Know what you owe

List every balance, interest rate, instalment and due date. Avoid estimating from memory; use actual statements.

Prioritise expensive debt

Higher-cost debt generally grows faster. Keep minimum payments current and direct extra money according to a clear repayment strategy you can sustain.

Warning signs

Missing payments, borrowing for groceries, using one account to pay another or avoiding statements are signals to act early rather than waiting for the situation to fix itself.

Get legitimate help

Be cautious of anyone promising instant debt removal for an upfront fee. For serious debt problems, use regulated and verifiable channels.
