Insurance Basics

Insurance transfers certain financial risks to an insurer in exchange for a premium, subject to the policy terms.

OverviewDocumentsBankingInsuranceConsumerWorkplaceWillsPrivacy

Premium

The premium is what you pay to keep cover active. Cheaper cover may have higher excesses, narrower benefits or more exclusions.

Excess

The excess is the amount you may have to contribute toward a claim. Know whether you could afford it.

Disclosure

Answer application questions accurately. Incorrect or incomplete information can create serious problems when you claim.

Review cover

As your life changes, old cover may become too little, unnecessary or incorrectly structured. Review policies instead of forgetting them for years.
