University

A degree can be valuable, but the right question is not simply 'Can I get in?' It is also whether the course and cost make sense for your goal.

OverviewUniversityTVETLearnershipsFundingInternshipsUpskilling

Research the qualification

Look at modules, admission requirements, accreditation where relevant, duration, practical components and what graduates actually do afterward.

Compare institutions

Consider total cost, location, accommodation, transport, support services and whether the qualification is recognised for the career you want.

Apply early

Application windows and document requirements differ. Use official institution websites and note deadlines yourself rather than relying only on forwarded posts.

Have a backup route

A second-choice course, another institution, TVET or a structured gap plan can be better than waiting a full year with no plan at all.
