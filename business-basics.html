Business Basics

A business can make sales and still lose money. Start by understanding the basic numbers.

OverviewBasicsPricingCIPCTaxRecordsCalculator

Revenue

Revenue is the money earned from sales before costs are deducted. A R20 000 month of sales is not automatically R20 000 of profit.

Costs

Separate direct costs such as stock or materials from overheads such as data, transport, rent, packaging and software.

Profit

Profit is what remains after relevant costs. You need enough profit to pay yourself, reinvest and absorb slow months or mistakes.

Cash flow

A profitable business can still run out of cash if customers pay late or too much money is tied up in stock. Timing matters as much as totals.

Fictional example · Naledi

R15 000 in sales is not R15 000 profit

Sales revenueR15 000
Stock− R7 000
Packaging & delivery− R1 800
Platform/payment fees− R900
Advertising & data− R1 000
Estimated profit before tax/other costsR4 300

Key idea: Revenue tells Naledi what customers paid. Profit tells her what may actually be left after costs.
