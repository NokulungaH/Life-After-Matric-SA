SARS & tax

eFiling Basics

SARS eFiling is the online portal used to manage many tax matters. Treat access to it like access to online banking.

OverviewTax numberIRP5eFilingReturnsAuto-assessment

Set up safely

Use the official SARS website or app, a strong unique password and your own contact details. Be cautious of links sent by SMS or email.

What you can do

Depending on your tax profile, eFiling can be used to view returns and assessments, update certain details, submit documents and manage correspondence.

Read before accepting

Do not click through an assessment just because the system presents one. Compare the information with your own records first.
