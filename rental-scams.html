Rental Scam Red Flags

Rental scams exploit urgency and the fear of losing a good property. Slow down before sending money.

OverviewRentingLeaseAffordabilityUtilitiesScams

Price that makes no sense

A property far below comparable rentals can be bait. Verify the listing independently rather than trusting screenshots or forwarded messages.

Pressure to pay before viewing

Be cautious when someone insists a deposit must be paid immediately before you can view or verify the property.

Verify the person and property

Check the agency or landlord, the address and available public information. If an agent is involved, use independently obtained contact details to confirm them.

Protect documents

Rental applications can involve sensitive documents. Watermark copies where appropriate and avoid sending full identity or banking documents to unverified contacts.
