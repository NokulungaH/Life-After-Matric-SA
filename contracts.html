Understand Your Employment Contract

Before you focus on the excitement of an offer, make sure you understand what you are agreeing to.

Overview CV Applying Interviews Contracts Job scams First job
South African baseline

The Basic Conditions of Employment Act requires employers, when employment starts, to provide certain employment particulars in writing. These include information such as the employer, the work, place of work, start date, ordinary hours, pay, overtime rate where applicable, deductions, leave and notice or contract period.

💵

Pay

Check whether the amount is monthly, hourly or annual, when you are paid, and whether the figure shown is basic salary or a total package.

🕐

Hours

Know your ordinary working days and hours, and understand how overtime or additional hours are handled if they apply to your role.

🏖️

Leave

Check annual leave, sick leave and any company-specific leave policy that forms part of your employment terms.

➖

Deductions

Understand what may be deducted from your pay and which benefits require employee contributions.

📆

Notice & duration

Know whether the job is permanent or fixed-term, whether there is probation, and what notice rules are stated.

📎

Policies

If the contract refers to another policy, handbook or document, ask where you can read it before assuming what it says.

Before you sign
  • Read every page, including annexures
  • Ask for unclear terms to be explained
  • Keep your own copy
  • Check that verbal promises appear in writing where appropriate
  • Do not sign blank pages or forms you do not understand

Official reference: Basic Conditions of Employment Act and Department of Employment and Labour — Basic Conditions of Employment. Last content review: September 2026.
