Wills & Beneficiaries

Planning for death feels far away when you are young, but basic decisions can prevent confusion for the people you leave behind.

OverviewDocumentsBankingInsuranceConsumerWorkplaceWillsPrivacy

A will

A valid will records how you want your estate dealt with, subject to South African law. Formal requirements matter, so do not assume an unsigned note is enough.

Beneficiary nominations

Retirement funds, insurance and other products can have beneficiary processes that do not work exactly like an ordinary bank account. Keep nominations current.

Keep people informed

Trusted people should know where important documents are kept and who to contact, without necessarily sharing private financial passwords.

Review after life changes

Marriage, divorce, children, property ownership or major financial changes are good reasons to review estate planning.
