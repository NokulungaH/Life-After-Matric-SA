Business & side hustles

Business Tax Basics

Once money is earned from business activities, tax and record-keeping questions start to matter even if the business is small.

OverviewBasicsPricingCIPCTaxRecordsCalculator

Income still matters

Calling income a side hustle does not automatically make it tax-free. Tax treatment depends on the taxpayer, structure and facts.

Company vs individual

A company has its own tax obligations, while a sole proprietor generally includes business income and allowable expenses in the individual’s tax affairs.

VAT

VAT registration has separate rules and thresholds. Do not charge VAT just because you have a company unless you are properly registered and entitled to do so.

Keep evidence

Invoices, receipts, bank records and expense evidence make tax compliance far easier than trying to recreate an entire year from memory.

Tax thresholds and filing rules change. Confirm current requirements directly with SARS or a qualified tax professional.
