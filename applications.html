Applying for Jobs

A strong application is not about sending the most CVs. It is about making it obvious why you match this specific role.

Overview CV Applying Interviews Contracts Job scams First job
🔎

Read the advert properly

Separate must-have requirements from preferences. Note the location, closing date, contract type, experience requested and how the employer wants applications submitted.

🧩

Match your evidence

Use the same honest terminology the employer uses when it accurately describes your skills. If they ask for Excel, do not hide Excel under a vague “computer skills” heading.

✉️

Keep the message short

When emailing a CV, state the role, introduce yourself briefly and say why your background is relevant. Attach the requested documents with clear file names.

🌐

Use LinkedIn sensibly

Make your headline and About section clear, keep dates consistent with your CV and engage professionally. You do not need to post every day to have a useful profile.

📌

Keep your own record

Record the employer, role, closing date and where you found it. Keep the advert too, because it may disappear before your interview.

↩️

Follow up carefully

If the advert gives a timeline, respect it. Otherwise a short, polite follow-up after a reasonable period is enough. Avoid repeated messages.

Simple application email

Subject: Application — Junior Test Analyst

Hello [Name], I’m applying for the Junior Test Analyst role advertised on [source]. My background in [relevant area] and experience with [relevant tools/skills] align with the role. I’ve attached my CV and the requested documents for consideration. Kind regards, [Name].
